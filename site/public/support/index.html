Common questions

A question was marked wrongly.
Marking is automatic and can be wrong. Report it from the question inside the app, which sends us the question and your answer; or write to us with the chapter and what you answered. A mark inside Pri Learning has no standing outside it.
Cancelling, renewals and refunds.
See the cancellation and refund policy. A subscription bought through the App Store is managed, cancelled and refunded in your Apple account, under Apple's terms.
Deleting an account.
In the app, or without it: how to delete your account. Deletion is immediate.
A student under 18.
Pri checks a child's answers only after a parent or guardian has confirmed the account by a link or a code sent to them. The same link or code lets them withdraw at any time, which stops marking, reading and syncing at once. See Children in the privacy notice.
Seeing, correcting or exporting your data.
The app exports what it holds about you. For anything else, write to the address above; the grievance process below applies. See Your rights.

Grievances

Last updated: 19 October 2026

If something about your data or your subscription is wrong, tell us. This page says who reads it and what happens next.

Publishing this contact is required of us by India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.

What to include

  • The email address on your account, if you have one.
  • What happened, and when.
  • What you would like us to do.

You do not need to quote the law or use any particular form.

What we do

  1. We acknowledge your message within 3 working days.
  2. We investigate and reply with an answer or a plan within 30 days, which is the period the DPDP Act allows.
  3. If we need longer, we say why and when we will answer.

For a request to see, correct or delete your data, we act within the same period. Deletion is permanent.

If we do not resolve it

You may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. You are expected to have raised it with us first, which is what this page is for.

Subscriptions

For billing questions write to impriysharan@icloud.com; see the refund policy. If a payment provider handled the charge, you may also raise it with them.
