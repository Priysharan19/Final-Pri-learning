How a question is marked

Three parts, kept separate on purpose. A reader proposes what you wrote; a fixed engine decides whether it is right; you can see and correct the step in between.

1

You write

With an Apple Pencil, a finger or the keyboard, one line of working at a time. You can also photograph working done on paper, or a textbook question to practise questions like it.

2

Pri reads it

A picture of your writing goes to Pri's server, which passes it to OpenAI to read. This is on for a signed-in account, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Handwriting, after which you type your answers instead. When you write with a pencil or a finger, the picture is drawn from your own strokes and nothing else: not the question, not the expected answer, not your name. A photograph is your camera's picture, so whatever is in the frame is sent. Pri keeps no copy of either.

You see the transcription and can correct it before it counts.

3

The rules decide

The mark is decided by Pri's marking engine, a fixed set of mathematical rules, never by an AI model. Marking is automatic and can be wrong: if a question is marked wrongly, report it from the question and we will look at it. A mark inside Pri Learning has no standing outside it.

Privacy, plainly

The privacy notice says exactly what is collected, where it is stored and for how long. The short version:

No ads. No tracking. Not on this site, not in the app. We do not sell your data.

An account, and a connection, to check answers. Work done without a connection is kept on your device until it can be checked.

Under 18? A parent or guardian confirms first. They get a link or a code, and can withdraw at any time.

Your handwriting is read by OpenAI while you are signed in. You can turn it off. Pri keeps no copy. Handwriting goes as a picture drawn from your strokes and nothing else: not the question, not the expected answer, not your name. A photograph sends whatever is in the frame, so keep out of shot what you would not want sent.

Delete your account whenever you like. In the app, or without it. Deletion is immediate.

Questions and grievances. A named grievance officer, a reply within 3 working days, and the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve it. See Support.
