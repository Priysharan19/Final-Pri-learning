Cancellation and refund policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers subscriptions bought from Priysharan Tripathi through the website. Subscriptions bought inside the iPad app are handled by Apple under its own refund policy, and are cancelled and refunded through your Apple account.

Cancelling

You can cancel a website subscription at any time from Settings → Account.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your subscription stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for, and then returns to the free plan. Your work, history and progress are not deleted when a subscription ends.

Refunds

  • Within 7 days of a first purchase, if you have not used more than a nominal amount of the paid features, we refund in full on request.
  • A renewal you did not intend: tell us within 7 days of the charge and we refund it, provided the new period has been largely unused.
  • A service fault that made the paid features unusable for a significant part of a period: we refund that part.

We do not refund a period you have used, or a subscription cancelled part-way through a period, except in the cases above.

How to ask

Write to impriysharan@icloud.com from the email address on the account, with the date of the charge. We reply within 3 working days and, where a refund is due, issue it to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days. Your bank may take longer to show it.

Disputes

If you are not satisfied with our answer, see Grievances.
