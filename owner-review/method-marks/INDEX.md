## Not available yet

These question types had a draft in an earlier version of this packet. They are NOT available now: the marked page the student sees cannot verify one of their criteria line by line, and the server pays only from that page, so nothing about them can be approved or paid. Each reason was checked by running a plausible correct transcript.

- Find the x-coordinates of the stationary points (Year 12 Mathematics Advanced; families `y12-appdiff`): The page leaves the derivative line and the "let the derivative equal zero" line unchecked (it checks the equation and the roots only), so two of the three criteria cannot be decided from the lines the student sees.

## Could not be given a provable scheme

These types were tried. A scheme is not offered because at least one criterion cannot be decided by the engine from the student's lines. Each reason was checked by running a plausible correct transcript.

- Trigonometric equations over a stated interval (Extension 1) (Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1; families `me12-trigeq`): The line that reduces the equation and the lines that list the angles are not read, so "reduces to a basic equation" and "finds all the solutions in range" cannot be decided.
- Binomial probability (Extension 1) (Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1; families `me12-binomial`, `me12-binomial-forms`): There is no step plan for this form and the formula and substitution lines are not read, so only the final value can be checked.
- Integration by substitution (Extension 1) (Year 12 Mathematics Extension 1; families `me12-calc`, `mex-integration`): A substitution line "u = ..." is a choice of notation and the transformed integral is not read, so no stage can be decided.
- The reverse chain rule: find the constant that makes an antiderivative true (Year 12 Mathematics Advanced; families `y12-integration`): The question gives the form of the antiderivative and asks for one constant, and a line such as "u = x^2 + 7" is a choice of notation, so no stage of the method can be decided from the lines; only the final value is checked. (A definite integral, or the area under a curve, now has a scheme: its antiderivative line is checked by differentiating it. A question that asks for the antiderivative itself is answered in one line, so a second criterion would pay that line twice.)
- Nature of a stationary point (Year 12 Mathematics Advanced; families `y12-appdiff`): The nature question is multiple choice, and the second derivative is not a line the page can check, so "determines the nature" cannot be decided from the lines.
- Applications of differentiation: greatest or least value (rates and optimisation) (Year 12 Mathematics Advanced; families `y12-appdiff`): The derivative of a function named P (or any capital-letter name) is not read, and the line that sets it to zero is not recognised, so the calculus steps cannot be decided.
- Exponential growth and decay models, and logarithmic equations (Year 12 Mathematics Advanced; families `y12-explogcalc`, `y12-func-model`, `y11-explog`): The lines that set up and solve the model equation (taking logarithms, finding k) are not read, so no method criterion can be decided.
- Arithmetic and geometric sequences and series (Year 12 Mathematics Advanced; families `y12-series`): The formula line and the substitution line are not read, and the question has no engine plan, so only the final value can be checked.
- Financial mathematics: compound interest, annuities and loans (Year 12 Mathematics Advanced; families `y12-financial`, `y12-fin-exact`): Only the final amount is checked. The formula and the substitution lines are not read, so no method criterion can be decided.
- Probability, random variables and the normal distribution (Year 12 Mathematics Advanced; families `y12-stats`, `y12-random-var`): The formula line (z-score, E(X), Var(X)) is not read, and a substitution line is checked only as arithmetic, not as the right substitution into the right formula, so "uses the correct formula" cannot be decided.

## One rule for every multi-mark written question

Besides the per-type schemes above, one generic rule can pay step marks for a wrong or missing final answer on most multi-mark written Year 11 and Year 12 questions, from each question's own authored worked solution. It is a separate approval, not one of the types above, and it is not approved. Read [generic-solution-steps.md](generic-solution-steps.md).
