# F4b · Exam Desk — screenshots

Browser-viewport screenshots (Playwright Chromium) of the Exam Desk built with
`PRI_FEATURE_LIBRARY=1 PRI_FEATURE_EXAM_DESK=1`, against the real platform
server on a throwaway SQLite database, as the Priyabhata edition.

**These are not device evidence.** No physical iPad, iPhone or Apple Pencil was
used; sizes are emulated viewports (phone 390 × 844, iPad landscape 1180 × 820,
iPad portrait 820 × 1180), light and dark.

The paper shown is the library's one seeded paper, offered to this edition at
the test's desk (no India paper is published yet). Elapsed times were produced
by moving the server's own clock stamps back in the test database. The import
preview reads the synthetic fixture
`client/test/fixtures/prithagoras-desk-export-v1.synthetic.json`.
