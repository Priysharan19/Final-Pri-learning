# Storyboard — "The Tangent" (36 s master, 1080×1920, 60 fps)

**Creative platform:** A student who understands can solve a problem they have never seen; a student who memorised cannot.

**The one idea:** The whole film is a single mathematical object — the tangent line — seen four ways: as a formula memorised without meaning (Act I), as an idea genuinely understood (Act II), as the same mathematics at four pressures from Class 7 to Olympiad (Act III), and as an invitation (Close). Every shot either serves that idea or was cut.

**Grid:** 120 BPM. One beat = 0.5 s = 30 frames. Every cut lands on a beat. 36.00 s = 2160 frames.

**Camera:** everything lives in a virtual 3D stage; no frame is perfectly static. Act I is locked-off and mechanical (the factory doesn't drift — rigidity is the point; the only motion is the machine's). Act II onward the camera breathes: slow drift, parallax between layers, shallow depth of field with meaningful focus pulls.

**Grade:** one grade across the whole film — lifted blacks (nothing below #0A0C10), gentle S-curve contrast, highlight roll-off on the accent, near-invisible animated grain, vignette. Sound-off first: every spoken line is a designed caption.

## Act structure

### S1 — HOOK · 0.00–1.50 (frames 0–90)
Black. At 0.10 s a sub-thump; the line **"Stop memorising maths."** sets itself in three
hard beats — "Stop" (0.10), "memorising" (0.35), "maths." (0.60) — huge display type,
tight leading — and on the third beat a gold line sweeps in under the words: the
tangent, promised before it is explained. Push-in ~4 % so the frame is alive. Grain and vignette already present: the film look starts at frame 0.
*Frame 0 is the cover: the full line already set, before the type re-slams for motion
viewers — cover exports from the composed end-state of this shot.*

- Audio: sub hit, dark room drone. Tick-tick of a metronome fades in late in the shot.
- Caption = the headline itself (no separate caption band).

### S2 — THE FACTORY · 1.50–8.00 (frames 90–480)
The problem, shown not stated. A conveyor of identical formula cards stamps into a
strict grid, one per beat — mechanical ease (things *snap*, no organic settle). The
cards are real, correct formulas set in real maths type — among them, dead centre,
**d/dx x² = 2x** (this exact card returns in Act II). Behind, faint rank-list rows
tick over: BATCH 7A · BATCH 7B · identical row after identical row.

- 1.50–4.50: stamp, stamp, stamp. Overline: **"Memorise. Repeat."** (from 2.0).
- 4.50–6.50: the grid multiplies — rows of the *same* card now, sameness at scale.
  Two-line beat: **"Four hundred formulas."** / **"No ideas."** (the planned
  strikethrough beat was cut in pass 1 — the two-line set carries it better).
- 6.50–8.00: **THE SEIZE.** A question card slides up that matches no pattern:
  *"Prove that eˣ ≥ 1 + x for every real x."* The stamping halts mid-motion, one card
  frozen at a wrong angle. Music cuts to near-silence — room tone only. Caption, small:
  **"Until the question is new."** Almost nothing moves for a full second. (First
  stillness beat.)

- Audio: quantised 16th-note tick, industrial thunk on every stamp, pitch-static —
  deliberately lifeless. At 6.50 everything stops dead; a single detuned tail rings out.

### S3 — THE TURN · 8.00–20.00 (frames 480–1200) — the product reveal
This is the hero object. Apple revolves aluminium; we revolve an idea.

- 8.00–10.00 · *The curve.* Out of the near-silence a warm bloom: a single lit curve —
  y = x² — draws itself in dark space, key-lit, rim-lit, shallow DOF, camera drifting
  in. It is the formula card from the factory, but alive.
- 10.00–15.00 · *The hinge shot.* Two points on the curve at x = 1 and x = 1 + h. The
  secant between them, its slope live: h = 1 → slope 3 · h = 0.5 → 2.5 · h = 0.1 → 2.1.
  The second point slides home with real mass (slow in, spring settle), the secant
  sweeps into tangency, and at **12.60 s** — on the beat — the tangent *locks*: the
  quotient (f(1+h) − f(1))/h resolves to **f′(1) = 2**. The equation racks into focus
  as the curve softens behind it. This is the loudest moment of the film and it is
  earned by the silence before it. Caption: **"The derivative isn't a rule. It's what
  you're watching."**
- 15.00–17.50 · *The ideal product.* The seize question returns — inside Pri Learning.
  The interface doesn't offer a formula sheet; it offers the idea: the tangent to eˣ at 0
  is y = 1 + x, and the curve never dips below its tangent. The student's handwritten
  stroke writes the closing line in real ink. Caption: **"Understand one idea. Solve what
  you've never seen."**
- 17.50–20.00 · *Marked.* Examiner ticks land on the handwritten proof, step by step,
  on beats — ✓ tangent at 0 · ✓ convexity · ✓ conclusion — then **"Marked like an
  examiner."** The product's real signature (live handwriting marking), shown at the
  moment it means the most.

- Audio: bloom pad from silence at 8.0; single felt-piano motif; swell into 12.6 where
  the tangent lock lands on a full warm chord (the dynamic-range moment); soft organic
  pulse enters at 15.0; two pencil-tick foley hits per ✓.

### S4 — THE INSTRUMENT · 20.00–26.50 (frames 1200–1590) — the feature run
The flex, inside the argument. Six lit panels of real product surface fly
through a depth corridor, each crossing the focal plane on a beat
(20.5 · 21.5 · 22.5 · 23.5 · 24.5 · 25.5), sharpening as they cross, dying
before they crowd the frame:

1. **Question bank** — the counter rolls to 3,44,798 (en-IN; the measured figure the shipped marketing quotes).
2. **Knowledge map** — the constellation draws itself: every topic, one web.
3. **Priorities** — the anti-factory feature, in the app's own voice: "Not quite. You keep flipping the sign when terms move." → misconception traced.
4. **Predicted percentile** — 96.4 (illustrative) with the launch reel's disclaimer: "estimate from your practice data — not a guarantee of results."
5. **Match Mode** — three race lanes, You in cream with the ♛ (rivals illustrative).
6. **Full mocks** — a criteria fragment ✓2 ✓2 ✗1 → "4 / 5 · method marks kept."

Then the run's punchline, set alone as the corridor empties: **"All of it offline."**

- Audio: the film's forward gear — eighth-note bass groove with off-beat hats,
  a whoosh + soft hit on every crossing, full strip-back under the offline line,
  riser into the ladder.

### S5 — THE LADDER · 26.50–33.00 (frames 1590–1980)
One continuous upward camera move — not four products, one ladder. Five stations rise
past, each the *same motif* (a line touching a curve) at a different pressure
(station rhythm escalates: Class 7 and Olympiad breathe, the middle compresses;
moves at 27.7 · 28.8 · 29.9 · 31.1):

1. **Class 7** — proportion: y = 2x, y always double of x.
2. **Class 10** — a tangent to a circle, radius ⊥ tangent at the point of contact.
3. **Class 12** — the derivative: f′(x) = 2x (the card, finally understood).
4. **JEE** — tangent to y² = 4x at (1, 2): x − y + 1 = 0.
5. **Olympiad** — the tangent-line trick: eˣ ≥ 1 + x — *the seize question, now a
   two-line proof.* The film answers its own opening wound.

Caption, held across the move: **"Class 7 to Olympiad. The same mathematics, at
different pressures."**

- Audio: rising figure, one step per station, building but understated; a riser into the close.

### S6 — CLOSE · 33.00–36.00 (frames 1980–2160)
The wordmark sets. Below it: **"Join the change."** Then, small: **@pri.learning ·
Coming soon.** Everything nearly still — the second stillness beat; the camera's drift
decays to zero. Fade to pure black by 35.70 so the last frame hands off cleanly to
frame 0. (Loop-safe: both ends black.)

- Audio: resolve chord, long decay, silence by 35.5.

## 15 s cut (900 frames) — a real edit, not a truncation
The 15 has no room for the factory to breathe, so it inverts the order of revelation:
it opens *on the curve already drawing* (beauty first — the scroll-stopper), states the
problem in one line, and lets the tangent lock carry everything.

- 0.00–1.00 · Curve drawing in the dark, already gorgeous. Type: **"Stop memorising maths."**
- 1.00–3.50 · Compressed factory stamps — the d/dx card among them — then the
  seize card at 2.5, one beat of stillness.
- 3.50–8.50 · Secant → tangent, lock at **7.00 s**.
- 8.50–10.50 · The product marks the handwritten close.
- 10.50–12.50 · Ladder as one lateral move through the five stations.
- 12.50–15.00 · Wordmark · "Join the change." · fade to black. Loop-safe.

Own audio mix (same palette, re-cued to the 0.5 s grid): seize silence after 2.5,
lock hits at 7.0.

## Mathematics on screen (all verified numerically in `src/math`, gated in `scripts/check.ts`)
- ((1+h)² − 1)/h = 2 + h → secant slopes 3, 2.5, 2.1 for h = 1, 0.5, 0.1; limit 2. ✓
- d/dx x² = 2x; tangent to y = x² at (1,1) is y = 2x − 1. ✓
- Tangent to eˣ at x = 0 is y = 1 + x; eˣ − (1+x) ≥ 0 with equality only at 0 (convexity). ✓
- Tangent to y² = 4x at (1,2): 2y·y′ = 4 ⇒ y′ = 2/y = 1 ⇒ x − y + 1 = 0; (1,2) lies on the curve (4 = 4). ✓
- Circle: radius ⊥ tangent at point of contact (Class 10, NCERT Ch. 10 Thm 10.1). ✓
