Pri reel — local video export

Encodes the frames rendered by tools/render-frames.mjs (WebCodecs H.264 + AAC, muxed to MP4) and PUTs the file to /export/. Chromium taints canvases for foreignObject SVG, so frames must come from the headless-Chrome render pass, not in-page rasterization. Params: ?name=out.mp4&bitrate=25000000.
