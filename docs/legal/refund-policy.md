# Cancellation and refund policy

**Last updated: 2 November 2026**

This policy covers Pri Learning Premium subscriptions. They are sold only
through Apple's App Store, on iPhone and iPad, and through Google Play, on
Android. There is no purchase on the website. The store takes your payment, so
the store's own refund rules apply alongside this policy. This policy has not
yet been reviewed by a lawyer; the app says so above it.

## Cancelling

You cancel in the store, not inside Pri Learning:

- **iPhone and iPad:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pri Learning →
  Cancel Subscription.
- **Android:** Play Store → your profile → Payments and subscriptions →
  Subscriptions → Pri Learning → Cancel subscription.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. Your subscription stays active until the end
of the period you have already paid for, and then returns to the free plan. Your
work, history and progress are not deleted when a subscription ends.

Deleting your Pri Learning account does not cancel the subscription. Cancel it
in the store first, or the store will keep renewing it.

## Refunds

**A purchase through the App Store is refunded by Apple, not by us.** We cannot
see your payment or issue the refund ourselves. Ask Apple at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) or from your Apple
account; Apple decides under its own policy, and we will support a request that
falls within the cases below if Apple asks us.

**A purchase through Google Play** can be refunded by Google directly within 48
hours of the charge, from your Play order history. After that, write to us and
we refund through Google Play in the cases below.

The cases in which we refund, or ask the store to:

- **Within 7 days of a first purchase**, if you have not used more than a
  nominal amount of the paid features, in full on request.
- **A renewal you did not intend**: tell us within 7 days of the charge and we
  refund it, provided the new period has been largely unused.
- **A service fault** that made the paid features unusable for a significant
  part of a period: we refund that part.

We do not refund a period you have used, or a subscription cancelled part-way
through a period, except in the cases above. Nothing here takes away a right you
have under Indian consumer law or under the store's own policy.

## How to ask

Write to impriysharan@icloud.com from the email address on the account, with the
date of the charge and which store took it. We acknowledge within 48 hours and
reply within 3 working days. A refund we issue through Google Play usually
shows on the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days; your bank may
take longer. For an App Store purchase, Apple tells you its decision directly.

## Disputes

If you are not satisfied with our answer, see [Grievances](/grievance). You may
also raise it with the store that took the payment.
