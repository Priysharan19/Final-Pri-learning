## What the app is, and is not

Prithagoras generates practice questions, reads handwriting, marks answers and
estimates progress. It is a practice tool.

Handwriting and photos are read by an outside service, OpenAI, as the
[privacy notice](/privacy) describes. The mark is always decided by our own
marking engine, never by that service, and you can correct what it read before
the answer counts.

**Marking is of the final answer only.** The app does not award the marks an
examiner gives for working, and it does not mark the way an HSC examiner would.
It reads your working and may point to a line where it goes wrong, but that is
feedback, not a mark.

Anything the app shows about your progress is an estimate from your own
practice. It is not a prediction of an HSC mark, band or ATAR, and nothing here
is a guarantee of any result.

Marking is automatic and can be wrong. If you believe a question was marked
incorrectly, tell us, because it helps us fix it; but a mark inside Prithagoras
has no standing outside it.
