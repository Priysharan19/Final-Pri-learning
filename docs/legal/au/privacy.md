# Privacy notice

**Last updated: 18 November 2026**

This notice explains what Prithagoras (Pri Learning's Australian edition)
collects, why, who else sees it, and what you can ask us to do about it. It is
written for students and parents in Australia. It is set out to give you what
the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 ask a privacy
policy to give you. We aim to handle your information in line with the APPs; how
far the Act applies to a business of our size is a question for a lawyer, and it
has not yet been reviewed by one. The app says so above this notice, and will
stop saying so only when that review has happened.

The person responsible for your information is
Priysharan Tripathi trading as Pri Learning, ABN 20 192 090 394.

## The short version

**You need a Pri Learning account, and a connection, to have answers checked.**
Marking is done online by our server: it issues each question, and its marking
engine, a fixed set of mathematical rules and not an AI model, decides the mark
for a signed-in account. In Prithagoras the mark is for the final answer only;
it does not award marks for working. A copy of that engine runs on your device
only to show your notation as you write and to display results recorded by
earlier versions of the app; it never sets the mark for a new answer. Signed out
or offline, nothing is checked or marked: the work you type or write is kept on
your device until it can be checked.

**Your handwriting and photos are read online.** When you write an answer by
hand or attach a photo, a picture of it is sent to our server, which passes it
to OpenAI to read. This is on for a signed-in account and can be turned off, in
which case you type your answers instead. The first time, the app asks your
permission, naming OpenAI, and nothing is sent until you agree. A second
setting, off unless you turn it on, sends your working to the same service to
find where a mistake is. Both are described below.

**Your original handwriting is kept with your marks.** When you submit a
handwritten or photographed practice answer to be marked, we keep your original
handwriting or photo with the mark for 2 years, and then delete it
automatically. You can get it in your data export. Only you, and your class
teacher for work you hand in, can ever be allowed to open it. The app has no
screen that shows it yet. See "Your original handwriting and photos" below.

**Some of your information goes overseas.** The handwriting reading service, our
email delivery service and our payment processor are outside our control and are likely to handle
your information outside Australia. See "Sending information overseas".

Without an account you can open the app and look around, but nothing about your
learning is sent anywhere, and nothing is marked.

This notice does not say where your information is held until the first two

still shows them in braces is a template, and the app says so.

## What stays on your device

- Your profile: the name you typed, your school year and maths course, and your
  settings.
- A copy of every question you have answered, your answers, your marks and your
  history. The marks themselves are decided by our server.
- Your handwriting strokes. (When an answer is marked, its original is also
  kept on our server: see "Your original handwriting and photos".)
- Your streaks, badges and progress.

If you set a password on a profile, this information is encrypted at rest on the
device. A profile without a password is not encrypted. Whoever can unlock the
device can open a profile that has no password.

## What we collect if you create a cloud account

- **Who invited whom**, if you use an invite link: we keep a record that your
  account joined through another account's link (or that yours was used), your
  invite code, and the dates of the free trial it gave. We never ask for or keep
  a friend's email address or phone number; you share the link yourself.
- **Your email address**, to sign you in and to send verification and password
  reset links and one-time sign-in codes. The app does not use SMS for sign-in; if
  that changes, this notice will describe it first.
- **Your name**, as you typed it.
- **Your answers, for marking**: the question our server issued, the answer and
  working you submit, and the mark the server decided; your exam papers and
  results.
- **Learning events**: that you answered a question, which topic it belonged to,
  whether you were right, and how long you took.
- **A picture of your handwriting, when you are signed in.** Your strokes stay
  on your device. Handwriting is read online: a picture of your writing is sent
  to our server and read there. This is on by default for a signed-in account
  and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Handwriting, after which you
  type your answers; it stays off for an account under 15 until a parent or
  guardian has confirmed it, and it never runs without an account.

  **Nothing is sent until you agree.** The first time a picture would be sent,
  whether your handwriting, a photo of your working or a photo of a question, the
  app asks your permission once, naming OpenAI. If you say no, reading is turned
  off and you type your answers; if you turn it back on, you are asked again
  before the next picture. Your answer is kept on your device, for that profile,
  and the app asks again whenever what it tells you changes.

  When you write with a pencil or your finger, what is sent is an image drawn
  from your own stroke coordinates and nothing else: not the question, not the
  expected answer, not your name or profile.

  **The same setting also covers a photograph.** If you attach a photo of work
  done on paper, or a scanned PDF of it, that photograph is sent to be read by
  the same service. It is your camera's picture, resized and re-encoded but not
  otherwise altered, so whatever is in the frame is sent, which can include the
  printed question, a name written on the page, or anything else on the paper.
  Keep out of shot whatever you would not want sent, or type your working
  instead.

  Either way, our server passes it to an outside reading service, OpenAI,
  through its API. Reading it does not save it: the reading step does not write
  the picture to our database, our storage or our logs. What the reading step
  leaves is the text the reading service returned, and any correction you typed,
  which we keep for 90 days with the record of that answer. (When you then
  submit the answer to be marked, we keep your original: see the next items.) We
  ask the reading service not to store it. Under its own terms it
  does not use it to train its models, but it may hold a copy for a limited time
  to watch for abuse; we do not control that. Turning the setting off stops the
  next picture of your handwriting or working from being sent, and it stays off;
  a picture already sent cannot be recalled. **"Practise this" is separate:** a
  photo of a printed question, taken to find practice questions like it, goes to
  OpenAI the same way after the same one-time permission, but the Handwriting
  setting does not cover it. It is sent only when you use "Practise this", and
  that photo is not kept. What
  the reading service returns is a transcription of your writing, nothing more,
  and you can correct it before it counts: your mark is always decided by the
  marking engine on our server, never by the reading service.
- **Reading happens by itself after a pause.** Once you have agreed to the
  reader, are signed in and are online, the page you are writing is read
  automatically when your pen has rested for about 1.3 seconds, so you do not
  have to press Read. It is the same reading with the same rules: only the
  picture is sent, nothing is sent before you have agreed, and nothing is marked
  until you check the reading and confirm it. To turn it off, go to Settings →
  Reading my handwriting; you can still press Read my answer yourself. That
  choice is kept on your device. The reading service never sees the expected
  answer, and the AI never decides a mark.
- **Your original handwriting and photos, kept with each marked practice
  answer.** When you submit a handwritten or photographed practice answer to be
  marked, our server keeps your original with the mark, so that there is a record of
  exactly what was marked. For handwriting that is your pen strokes as your
  device recorded them (where the pen was, when, how hard it pressed, how it was
  tilted, and the strokes you erased). For a photo it is the photo or scanned
  page as you sent it. We also keep a fingerprint of it (a SHA-256 value), which
  shows it has not been changed; which strokes each line you confirmed came from;
  and the reading beside it: what Prithagoras read, the text you confirmed and
  any edits you made.
- **How long an original is kept:** 2 years (730 days) after it was kept. Then it is deleted
  automatically.
- **An original is deleted with your account**, and **included when you export your
  information**.
- **Who can open an original:** only you (it is in your data export). The app has
  no screen that shows it yet. If you are in a class and hand in work to your
  class teacher, our server will let that teacher open the original of the
  answers in the work you hand in, and nothing you have not handed in; the
  teacher's screen does not show it yet. No other student or teacher can open it.
  There is no screen in our administrator tools for opening it either, although
  we, as the operator, can reach the database it is stored in to run the
  service.
- **A limit on what is kept:** one account can have up to 40 MB of handwriting and 24 MB of
  photos kept. Past that, the answer is still marked, but its original is not
  kept on our server. Your device still has its own copy.
- **Reading is answer-blind and you confirm it.** The reading service sees
  only the picture, never the expected answer or the solution. You check what
  was read, and can correct it, before the answer counts. The reading never
  decides a mark.
- **Help from an AI explainer, when you ask for it.** When you open a hint
  step, a walk-through, "Why this step?", a check of a line of your working or a
  mistake explanation, or type a question to the tutor, our server sends what is
  needed to OpenAI: the question, our own worked solution to it (from our
  question bank, not from you), the lines of working or the answer you have
  written, and what you typed to the tutor. Not your name, email address or
  profile. Anything an AI wrote is labelled as written by AI and not checked by
  the maths engine, and the AI never sets a mark. A copy of a reply may be kept
  for up to 24 hours so that the same question is not asked twice; a reply to
  something you typed yourself is kept only for your account. We also record,
  for 90 days with the question, that you were given help on it, so that your
  progress can say whether you answered on your own or with help. It is on for a
  signed-in account whose guardian consent, where one is needed, is in place.
- **Your working and the question, only if you switch that on.** There is a
  second setting, also off unless you turn it on, that sends the lines of
  working you wrote and the text of the question when an answer is wrong and the
  app cannot tell you which line went wrong. It is used to tell you where the
  mistake is. Our server keeps no copy; the checking service handles it as the
  reading service above does. The expected answer is never sent with it, and your
  name and profile are never sent with it. Your mark is decided by the marking
  engine on our server either way.
- **A parent or guardian's name and email address**, only for an account of
  someone under 15, so we can send them the confirmation link or code described
  under Children and show you, partly hidden, where it went.
- **Device information** needed to keep you signed in: a random device
  identifier this app generates (it is not your device's own id and not anything
  you typed), a one-way hash of your browser's user-agent string, and the times
  you signed in and were last seen.
- **Crash reports**, while the Crash reports setting is on (it is on for a
  signed-in account and can be turned off for the device in Settings): the name
  of the screen that failed, a short error code, a fingerprint of the fault and
  the app's build number. Never the error message, the page address, the
  question, your writing or anything you typed.

**If you buy Premium**, you pay on a page hosted by Stripe, our payment
processor. You type your card details into Stripe's page, not ours: we never

## Cookies and storage

We use only what the app needs to work, and nothing to track you.

- **Sign-in cookies.** When you sign in, our server sets cookies that keep you
  signed in on that browser, protect your session against forged requests and,
  for a moment, show that you have just confirmed your password. They are removed
  when you sign out. They are not used to follow you around other sites.
- **Storage on your device.** The app keeps your profile, your work and your
  settings in your browser's or phone's own storage so it can open and keep your
  place. This is what "What stays on your device" describes.

We use no advertising or analytics cookies, no tracking pixels and no
third-party trackers.

## Children

Prithagoras is used by school students, many of them young.

**What the app does today, plainly: it asks your age but does not keep it, and
it never asks for your date of birth.** When you create an account it asks your
age (11 to 17, or 18 or older) and which school year and maths course you are
taking, which tells it what maths to set you. **A student under 15 needs a
parent or guardian to confirm the account. A student who is 15 or older does
not, and nor does an adult.** We keep only that decision (whether a guardian was
needed), not your age. We drew the line at 15 because Australian privacy
guidance is commonly read as treating a student under 15 as generally unable to
consent for themselves; whether that is the right line for us is one of the
questions we have for a lawyer. For an account under 15, the app asks for a
parent or guardian's name and email address. We email them a link to confirm the account, or a six-digit code, which
they enter on their own page, on their own device, after reading a short notice
of what they are agreeing to. Until they confirm, the account can sign in, show
and export what it holds, and be deleted, and nothing else: no answer is marked,
no handwriting or photo is read, and it does not sync, join a class, send a
report, send a crash report, subscribe, or use either server setting. The same
email lets them withdraw at any time, which stops all of that at once: marking,
reading, exams and sync. A withdrawn confirmation cannot be given again with the
same link.

**If no parent or guardian has confirmed the account 30 days after it was
created**, or 30 days after a parent or guardian withdraws, the account is
closed: it stops working and every sign-in on it ends. Everything it holds is
erased 7 days later. A parent or guardian can also ask us to erase the account
without the child's login, from the link in our email or by writing to the
address under "Your rights: access and correction"; it then stops working at
once and is erased 7 days later.

**What that confirmation proves, and what it does not.** It shows that someone
who can read that email inbox followed the link or entered the code. It does not
show that they are an adult, or that they are your parent or guardian. Our
record of it says which method was used, so nobody can mistake it for more than
it is.

If you are under 15, please set this up with a parent or guardian and show them
this notice, particularly the section on what a cloud account collects. Answers
cannot be checked without an account; without one, nothing about your learning
leaves the device.

**The OAIC's Children's Online Privacy Code.** We understand the Office of the Australian
Information Commissioner (OAIC) may issue a Children's Online Privacy Code. At
the date of this notice we have not treated any such Code as being in force, and
we do not claim to comply with one. We will review this notice and the sign-up
flow if a Code applies to us.

We do not use children's information for advertising, we do not track children
across other services, and we do not build behavioural profiles for anything
other than choosing the next maths question.

## Teachers and classes

A teacher can see you only if you join their class with its class code. Then,
and only while you are a member and your account is allowed to use the cloud:

- **A teacher can see:** your name (never your email address); that you are in
  the class and since when; for each syllabus outcome, where your server-marked
  work stands; counts of common mistakes across the class, with no names
  attached; papers you hand in to that teacher (the score, each question's marks,
  your working as read, line checks and where the first mistake was); the
  handwriting of the answers in a paper you hand in, where one is kept (our server
  allows it; the teacher's screen does not show it yet); and the marks and
  comments the teacher gives.
- **A teacher cannot see:** your email address, your guardian's details, your
  plan, your notes, your conversations with the AI tutor, your practice working,
  your photos or your practice handwriting, papers you have not handed in, or
  anything from other classes.
- **You can see the same list.** The app shows students what their teacher can
  see. If you leave the class, are removed from it, or a parent or guardian
  withdraws consent, the teacher stops seeing you at once.
- **Teacher marks.** If a teacher awards or declines a mark, it is recorded with
  which teacher made it and when, which criterion, how many marks, and the
  engine's mark at the time. It is kept apart from the engine's mark, added to
  the record and never written over it, and you can see it. An AI never suggests
  a teacher mark.

## Safety messages

If what you type to the tutor or another help feature suggests you may be
unhappy, frightened or in danger, the app does not send it to an AI and does not
tutor. It shows a calm message and the support line for your region, Kids
Helpline on 1800 55 1800, and asks you to tell a trusted adult near you. No one
is told that it happened, and the message is not stored. The check is a fixed
list of words and phrases, so it can miss things, and it is not a crisis
service. If you are in danger now, tell an adult near you or call your local
emergency number.

## Product measurement and experiments

We count, across all accounts together, how many accounts answered a question,
how many came back later, how many free trials became paid subscriptions, and
the optional one-tap reasons people give when they cancel. These counts come
from records we already hold, are read only by our administrator with a second
sign-in check, and are hidden when fewer than 10 accounts are behind a number.
The app has no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code: nothing in
it follows you to other sites.

We have built, but not switched on, a test of whether choosing the smallest next
hint by a fixed rule helps students more than the usual hint ladder. It is off.
If it were ever switched on, each account would be put in one of two groups by a
fixed calculation, the group would be noted on your answer records, and your
marks and credit would not change.

## How long we keep it

- Information on your device stays until you delete the profile or the app.
- Learning events on our server are kept while your account is open.
- The record of each marked answer (the question, your answer and working, the
  transcription of your handwriting or photo with any correction you typed, and
  the mark) is kept for 90 days. After that your answer, your working and the
  transcription are gone; which question it was, whether it was right, the marks
  and the topic stay in your learning history while your account is open.
- Your original handwriting or photo kept with a marked practice answer is
  deleted automatically 2 years (730 days) after it was kept, or when you delete
  your account, whichever comes first. The mark and your learning history stay
  while your account is open.
- Exam papers, the answers you gave in them and their results are kept for up to
  five years, or until you delete your account, whichever comes first.
- Telemetry, including crash reports, is limited to an allow-list of fields and
  is deleted after 90 days.
- An account of someone under 15 that no parent or guardian has confirmed 30 days
  after it was created, or 30 days after a parent or guardian withdrew, is closed
  then and erased 7 days later, as described under Children.
- When you delete your account it is deleted at once; there is no waiting
  period. That removes your profile, your sign-in details and every session, your
  synced work and answer records, your exam papers, your class memberships and
  submissions, your telemetry and any guardian's details. Your email address can
  then be used to create a new, empty account.
- Four things are kept after deletion, with nothing in them that identifies you:
  the record of each payment you made (Stripe's reference, the amount, the
  currency, the date and its status), kept without your name, email or account so
  that a later refund can still be matched to it;
  any problem report you sent about a question, with everything you typed into it
  removed; a log entry saying that an account was deleted, with no name or
  email; and the record of an invitation (an invite code, or an opaque account
  number), which no longer leads to you once your account is deleted.
- Backups of our database made before you deleted your account still contain it
  until they are replaced. Operational logs that contain no personal information
  may persist.

## Your rights: access and correction

You can ask us to (these requests follow the access and correction principles, APP 12 and APP 13, which we aim to follow):

- **Show you** what we hold about you. The app exports it directly, including
  your stored original handwriting and photos.
- **Correct** anything that is wrong.
- **Delete** your account and its information. You can do this inside the app, in
  Settings → Account, whichever way you sign in: with your password, with your
  Apple or Google sign-in, or with a code sent to your email address. If you no
  longer have the app, you can do it at
  [/account/delete-request](/account/delete-request) with a code sent to the
  account's email address. Either way the deletion is immediate, as described
  under "How long we keep it".
- **Withdraw consent**, after which we stop processing and delete.

Write to prilearning19@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge your message within 48 hours and
answer within 30 days. If we cannot give you access to something, or cannot
correct it, we will tell you why.

## Security

Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Sign-in tokens are stored as one-way
hashes. Verification and reset links are single-use, short-lived, and delivered
encrypted. One-time codes are never stored, only a keyed one-way hash of each
is, and they expire after ten minutes and five attempts. Connections use HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a data breach affecting
your information that is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you, and the
OAIC where the Privacy Act requires it.
