us. This page says who reads it and what happens next. It has not yet been
reviewed by a lawyer; the app says so above it.

## What to include

- The email address on your account, if you have one.
- What happened, and when.
- What you would like us to do.

You do not need to quote the law or use any particular form.

## What we do

1. We acknowledge your message within **48 hours**.
2. We investigate and reply with an answer or a plan within **30 days** of
   receiving it. If we need longer, we say why and when we will answer.

For a request to see, correct or delete your information, see "Your rights:
access and correction" in the [privacy notice](/privacy). Deletion you do
yourself, in the app or from the delete-request page, is immediate. Deletion is
permanent.

## If we do not resolve it

If your complaint is about how we handled your personal information, you may
complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at
oaic.gov.au. We would like the chance to resolve it first, which is what this
page is for.

If it is about a purchase or a consumer guarantee, you may also contact NSW Fair
Trading.
