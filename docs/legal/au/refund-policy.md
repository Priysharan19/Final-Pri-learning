# Cancellation and refund policy

**Last updated: 19 November 2026**

Prithagoras Premium is a subscription. This page says what it costs, who takes
the payment, how to cancel and how to ask for a refund. It has not yet been
reviewed by a lawyer; the app says so above it.

## Price

Premium is **A$9.99 a month** or **A$99 a year**, in Australian dollars (AUD).
Where GST applies to a purchase, the price you see includes it. This policy is
not a statement that we are registered for GST, and a receipt is not a tax
invoice unless it says it is. The subscription renews at the end of each month or
year until you cancel it.

## HSC Pass to the exams (when it is on sale)

The HSC Pass to the exams is a single payment with no renewal. The app shows the
exact price and the exact date it ends before you pay. It ends on that date by
itself and nothing more is charged. There is nothing to cancel. If the payment
for the pass is refunded in full, the Premium it gave ends at once. Your rights
under the Australian Consumer Law, below, apply to it as to any other purchase.

## Who takes the payment

Payments are taken by Stripe, our payment processor, on a payment page hosted by
Stripe. You type your card details into Stripe's page: we never receive or store a
card number. We keep Stripe's reference for your subscription, whether it is
active, and a record of each payment (Stripe's reference, the amount, the
currency, the date and its status). See the [privacy notice](/privacy).

## Cancelling

You can cancel in the app, from your account, at any time. Premium then stays on
until the end of the period you have already paid for, and is not renewed after
it. If you delete your account, any subscription is cancelled at once. Your
work, history and progress are not deleted when a subscription ends. If a payment
fails, Premium may stop when the period you have paid for ends.

## Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian
Consumer Law. If a service we provide fails to meet a consumer guarantee, you are
entitled to a remedy, which can include a refund. If the failure is a major
failure, you can choose a refund for the unused part of the service or
compensation for the drop in its value, and you are entitled to compensation for
any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. Nothing in this policy takes
away a right you have under the Australian Consumer Law, and we do not say or
imply that a purchase cannot be refunded.

## How to ask for a refund

Write to prilearning19@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the date
of the charge. We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours and reply within 3 business
days. Whatever we decide, you keep the rights described above. If a payment for
the current period is refunded in full, Premium for that period ends at once.

## Disputes

If you are not satisfied with our answer, see [Contact](/contact). You may also
raise it with NSW Fair Trading or Stripe.
