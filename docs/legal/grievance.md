India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 requires us to publish a
contact for your questions and complaints about your data. This is it.

## What to include

- The email address on your account, if you have one.
- What happened, and when.
- What you would like us to do.

You do not need to quote the law or use any particular form.

## What we do

1. We acknowledge your message within **48 hours**.
2. We investigate and reply with an answer or a plan within **30 days** of
   receiving it; where the law sets a shorter time for your kind of request,
   that shorter time applies.
3. If we need longer, we say why and when we will answer.

For a request to see, correct or delete your data, we act within the same
period. Deletion you do yourself, in the app or from the delete-request page,
is immediate. Deletion is permanent.

## If we do not resolve it

You may complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**. You are expected to
have raised it with us first, which is what this page is for.
