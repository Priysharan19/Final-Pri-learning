# AI-Era Assessment Integrity and Process Evidence V7

Status: V7 assessment/governance deep dive
Freshness: 1 October 2026

## 1. Purpose

Generative AI changes what an unsupervised answer proves.

If a learner submits a polished solution produced by an external model, the product may observe:

- a correct artifact;

without evidence that the learner personally:

- selected the method;
- executed the reasoning;
- verified the result.

Pri therefore needs assessment modes whose evidence claims match the actual conditions.

The answer is not universal surveillance.

The answer is **assessment design + explicit AI policy + process evidence + supervised independent checks where necessary**.

## 2. AI-era integrity is a validity problem

The deepest question is not:

> Did this student cheat?

It is:

> What claim about the learner is justified by this evidence under these conditions?

If external AI use is uncontrolled:
a take-home final answer may have low validity as evidence of independent competence.

That does not make the artifact worthless.

It changes what can be inferred.

## 3. Current review evidence favors assessment redesign over detection

A 2025 systematic review published in AI and Ethics in 2026 argues that AI-infused assessment requires redesign, emphasizing:

- process-based assessment;
- multi-stage assessment;
- oral/viva components;
- reflective/evaluative judgment;
- explicit AI-use policy.

Source:
https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-025-00871-w

Population limitation:
the review is primarily higher education.

Pri should transport the principle, not the exact methods.

## 4. AI-content detectors are not high-stakes proof

A 2026 systematic evaluation tested 13 AI-generated-content detectors on authentic student:

- assignments;
- theses;
- code.

The study found:
- systematic failures across tasks/disciplines;
- inadequate reliability for high-stakes assessment;
- adversarial/hybrid edits enabled much AI-generated content to evade detection.

Source:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105616

### Pri rule

Do not use an AI-text detector as sole or decisive evidence of misconduct.

For mathematics, this principle is even stronger because:
- short equations are hard to attribute;
- correct methods are naturally similar;
- structured notation is not stylistically unique.

## 5. Process evidence is more valuable than speculative authorship detection

In Pri, possible process evidence includes:

- question shown;
- first response;
- handwritten strokes;
- intermediate mathematical states;
- revisions;
- help requests;
- AssistanceEnvelope;
- timestamps;
- independent exit tasks.

This can support a stronger claim about the learner's process.

But process evidence still does not prove:
- nobody else helped;
- no external device was used.

Use evidence honestly.

## 6. Process evidence should not become surveillance exhaust

Record only what has educational or integrity value.

Do not require:
- webcam recording;
- gaze tracking;
- microphone;
- room scans;
- location

for ordinary Pri learning.

A 2026 systematic review of automated online proctoring identifies recurring concerns around:
- fairness;
- bias;
- interpretability;
- variable physical conditions.

Source:
https://doi.org/10.1007/s44217-026-01224-3

### Pri default

Prefer redesign and supervised institutional conditions over invasive consumer-device proctoring.

## 7. Assessment modes must be explicit

### LEARN
AI/tutoring may be available.
Evidence is formative.

### PRACTISE-INDEPENDENT
Pri help disabled.
External conditions not guaranteed.
Useful low-stakes independence evidence.

### PRI-VERIFIED SESSION
Pri controls its own assistance surface and records process.
Still cannot guarantee no external device/person unless supervised.

### SUPERVISED ASSESSMENT
Human/institution-controlled conditions.
Can support stronger independent-performance claims.

### AI-PERMITTED ASSESSMENT
AI use is explicitly part of the task.
Assessment measures:
- prompting/use;
- verification;
- judgment;
- mathematical output.

Do not merge these evidence classes.

## 8. Independent does not mean “AI detector says human”

Independent evidence means:

> under the declared assessment conditions, Pri has no recorded target-reasoning assistance and the assessment environment is sufficient for the claim being made.

The environmental confidence should be explicit.

## 9. Evidence strength object

Possible fields:

- assessment mode;
- supervision type;
- Pri assistance allowed?;
- external resources allowed?;
- calculator allowed?;
- notes allowed?;
- AI allowed?;
- process captured?;
- identity assurance;
- device assurance;
- evidence confidence.

A school-supervised exam and an unsupervised home quiz should not feed learner state identically.

## 10. High-stakes readiness should triangulate

Strong readiness evidence can combine:

- Pri independent tasks;
- protected transfer;
- school/teacher assessment;
- board/mock exam;
- supervised Pri session where available.

Do not rely on one unsupervised score.

## 11. Oral defense can verify understanding selectively

For suspicious or high-stakes cases, a short teacher-led oral explanation can ask:

- Why did you choose this method?
- What would change if this condition changed?
- Where is the critical step?

This can test actual understanding.

But Pri should not automatically accuse a learner and demand a viva because an AI detector fired.

## 12. Learner-generated process explanations

A reflective explanation can add evidence.

However:
LLMs can generate reflections too.

Therefore reflection alone is not authorship proof.

Its value is pedagogical and triangulatory.

## 13. “AI-resistant” should not mean artificially weird

Bad assessment redesign:
- obscure wording;
- trick context;
- unnecessary live performance;
- impossible time constraints.

These may reduce validity.

Better:
- strategy justification;
- local data/context;
- process checkpoints;
- parameter changes;
- oral follow-up;
- error analysis;
- transfer.

The mathematical construct stays central.

## 14. AI-completable is not automatically invalid

If the task goal is:
- use a tool to solve/verify;
- evaluate AI;
- model a problem;

AI may be legitimately permitted.

Assessment validity depends on the intended construct.

## 15. Separate authorship from competence

Two distinct questions:

### Authorship
Who produced this artifact?

### Competence
Can the learner perform the capability?

Pri primarily needs the second.

When authorship matters for school policy, teacher/institution remains authority.

## 16. AI-use declaration

For permitted AI tasks, store:
- AI allowed/not;
- learner declaration;
- tool class where relevant;
- what AI was used for.

Do not require more provider/account detail than necessary.

The declaration supports transparency, not perfect policing.

## 17. Assistance equivalence

External AI assistance and Pri assistance differ in observability.

Pri can record:
- exact hints.

External AI may be unknown.

Therefore learner state from unsupervised take-home work should carry lower independence confidence than controlled no-help tasks.

## 18. Protected transfer is especially important in the AI era

If outcome items are:
- publicly indexed;
- retrievable by external models;
- repeatedly reused;

they become weaker evidence.

Protected transfer bank should minimize:
- publication;
- tutoring retrieval;
- repeated exposure.

For formal board exams:
external authority supplies a separate validation layer.

## 19. Process-based measurement can improve learning as well as integrity

A 2026 educational-measurement review describes a broader shift toward process-oriented interpretations as AI becomes integrated into measurement.

Source:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2026.100789

### Pri consequence

Intermediate work is not only anti-cheating evidence.

It is the richest source for:
- first-break diagnosis;
- strategy;
- metacognition;
- feedback.

Integrity and pedagogy can align.

## 20. Avoid punitive automation

Pri should not automatically:
- accuse;
- penalize;
- notify guardian;
- lower mark

based on anomalous process patterns.

At most:
- flag evidence uncertainty;
- request independent check;
- route to human according to institutional policy.

## 21. Identity

For ordinary learning:
local profile continuity may be enough.

For formal assessment:
identity may be controlled by:
- school;
- teacher;
- institution.

Pri should not collect government ID merely to strengthen everyday learner-state confidence.

## 22. Device switching

A learner may:
- start on iPad;
- continue web.

That is not suspicious.

Integrity logic must not mistake legitimate offline/sync/device behavior for misconduct.

## 23. Accessibility

Assessment integrity controls must not penalize:
- screen reader;
- dictation;
- extended time;
- switch input;
- approved accommodations.

Accommodation metadata belongs in assessment policy.

## 24. Mathematics process fingerprints are not identity biometrics

Pri may model:
- common solution routes.

It should not use:
- handwriting style;
- timing pattern

as covert biometric identity verification unless a separate lawful, explicit policy exists.

## 25. Assessment-integrity event

Potential fields:

- assessment_session_id;
- mode;
- allowed_resources;
- Pri assistance;
- external-assistance declaration;
- supervision;
- process-evidence completeness;
- integrity anomaly;
- human review;
- final evidence class.

Anomaly does not equal misconduct.

## 26. Evidence downgrade rather than accusation

If process evidence is missing:

Instead of:
“cheating detected,”

use:
“independence not verified.”

This is epistemically accurate.

## 27. Teacher workflow

Teacher sees:

- evidence conditions;
- process completeness;
- unusual event if materially relevant.

Teacher decides:
- accept;
- request follow-up;
- supervised reassessment.

Pri does not adjudicate misconduct autonomously.

## 28. Research programme

### INT-1
Compare unsupervised result-only vs process-captured prediction of later supervised mathematics performance.

### INT-2
Measure whether short independent exit tasks improve validity over detector-style signals.

### INT-3
Study teacher judgments with/without process evidence.

### INT-4
Audit accessibility false flags.

### INT-5
AI-permitted assessment:
evaluate whether explicit verification tasks predict independent competence.

## 29. Success metric

The goal is not:
maximum cheating detection.

It is:

> valid, fair evidence of the learner capability Pri claims to measure.

## 30. Core decision

Pri should respond to generative AI by making mathematical judgment and learning process more visible, not by building an invasive automated proctor.

When independence cannot be verified, say so.

When stronger evidence is required, collect a stronger assessment.
