## False rejection: 2, open, not credit leaks

1. `c8-rational-numbers-strategy` d3: the right answer `-6/5`, written `(-6)/5`, is refused with a misconception message ("The last product is subtracted; keep its sign when you factor."). A trap value seems to equal the key, or the bracketed-numerator form is not read on a `requireFraction` question. A right answer must never be named as a misconception, so this needs a generator or trap fix in its own change.
2. `y7-algebra` d2: the key `0x + -8` is refused when written as `-8`, because the engine requires a variable when the key contains one. The real fault is the generator emitting a zero coefficient. That is a generator fix.

## Policy buckets: reported, not defects

- **POLICY_NOISE_OR_DISPLAY** (4,517): the engine's pinned allowances are a calculator display within 1e-9 of a whole-number key, and differences below binary64 noise.
- **POLICY_FORM_RULE** (3,117): declared form rules, such as unevaluated `+ 0` (D13), unsimplified fractions and unsimplified surds.
- **POLICY_STRICTER_APPROX** (850 on main): rounded decimals refused where the question names a precision or demands an exact or fractional value.
- **POLICY_CONSTANT_NOT_REQUIRED** (26): on `stripC` questions the engine accepts an antiderivative without `+ C`. This is a pedagogical choice for the owner and curriculum leads, not an equivalence question. It is recorded here so it is a decision, not an accident.

## What the oracle does not cover

MCQ, working/step marking, ratio, interval, matrix, vector and complex answers are out of scope: they are measured by their own suites. Handwriting is out of scope as well. The candidates are generated by rule, not written by students.
