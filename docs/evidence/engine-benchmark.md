## How to read this

- A false positive here is a wrong answer the marker called right and is release-blocking under the project's invariants ("wrong mathematical marking" preempts feature work); `--strict` makes this script exit non-zero on any.
- A false negative here is a canonical authored answer the marker refused; it is a defect in the generator's canonical form or the parser, and the row names the seed to reproduce it.
- The equivalence-class table is authored, not generated; its false-positive half (x < 2 for x > 2, 0.51 for 1/2, the conjugate, the transpose) is the part that matters most.
