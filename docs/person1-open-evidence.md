# Person 1 open evidence

The following cannot be closed by repository edits alone:

- collecting writer-disjoint Apple Pencil sessions from enough real people;
- scoring at least 1,000 real-Pencil test expressions;
- measuring safe-auto-mark precision on those readings;
- measuring p95 recognition latency on supported physical iPads;
- demonstrating coverage across representative handwriting styles and hardware.

These are release-validation tasks. The codebase must keep them visible as blockers until measurements exist.
