# India difficulty coverage — where the hardest level has no authored form

Generated by `node tools/india-difficulty-coverage.mjs` from the engine's own
`indiaRequestableDifficulties` (issue #408). Do not edit by hand; `--check` fails when it is stale.

**What this is.** A count of authored question *forms* per chapter × track × level. A level is
listed only when a form is authored at exactly that level inside the track's difficulty window.

**What this is not.** It is not evidence of human review, of past-paper provenance, of the number of
distinct reasoning archetypes at a level, or that a "D4" form reaches the depth of the examination a
track is named after. A cell that is not listed below has *a* form at the hardest level; it may be a
single routine template.

**What the product does with a gap.** The picker does not offer a level that has no authored form for
the chosen chapter or dot point. A request that still names one (an old link, a typed URL, a task) is
refused: no question is served at another level. The page says the level is unavailable, lists the
levels that do exist, and serves a question only after the student chooses one.
