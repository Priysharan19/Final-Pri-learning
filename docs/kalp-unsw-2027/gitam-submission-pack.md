# GITAM Innovation Challenge 2026 — Junior Track Submission Pack

## Gate before payment/submission

The official 2026 Junior Track is for Classes 10–12, teams of 2–4 students from the same school, and describes the expected stage as **Concept / Early Idea**. Pri Learning is already an existing student-built product, so obtain organiser confirmation before paying the entry fee.

Use this exact eligibility question:

> We are a Class 12 school-student team working on an already-built early-stage EdTech prototype that is currently being piloted with students. Is an existing early-stage prototype eligible for the Junior Track, or is the track restricted to concepts that have not yet been built?

Do not submit Pri Learning to this track until the answer is affirmative.

## 1-minute pitch

A mathematics student can practise hundreds of questions and still receive very little help understanding *why* a particular line of working went wrong.

I am Kalp Bajpai, a Class 12 student and [Founder/Co-Founder] of Pri Learning. We are building a mathematics-learning platform that combines syllabus-aligned practice, adaptive progression and feedback designed around students' mathematical working rather than only their final answers.

Our next step is evidence. I am leading a structured CBSE Class 10 pilot in which students complete a baseline assessment, use Pri Learning over several weeks, and complete a parallel post-assessment while we collect student and teacher feedback.

We want to use those findings to improve the product before scaling. Our long-term goal is to make high-quality personalised mathematics support more accessible to Indian students while complementing, not replacing, teachers.
