## Form-answer bank

### What problem are you solving?

Students often receive outcome-level marking without sufficiently personalised feedback on the mathematical reasoning behind an error. One-to-one support can address this, but it does not scale evenly across geography and household resources.

### Why does this matter in India?

India has an enormous and diverse student population. A scalable system that helps students practise independently, diagnose misconceptions and receive useful feedback could complement teachers and tutoring rather than replace them.

### What is innovative?

The focus is not merely generating questions or showing solutions. Pri Learning is being built around the student's working process: adaptive question selection, mathematical working, error/misconception feedback and longitudinal progress, with a local-first architecture.

### How will you test whether it is useful?

A four-week CBSE Class 10 mathematics pilot will use a syllabus-blueprinted baseline assessment, structured Pri Learning sessions, a parallel post-assessment and student/teacher feedback. Results will be anonymised and reported without causal overclaiming.

### How can it scale?

The platform is software-based and designed for repeatable mathematics practice. Scaling depends on proving product usefulness, widening curriculum coverage, improving device/platform reach and working with schools/educators rather than assuming adoption.
