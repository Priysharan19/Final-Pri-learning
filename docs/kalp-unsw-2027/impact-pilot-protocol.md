# Pri Learning India Student Impact Programme 2026

## Purpose

Run a genuine, ethical, independently verifiable pilot that can answer two questions:

1. What happens when a defined cohort of Indian secondary students uses Pri Learning for structured mathematics practice over four weeks?
2. What leadership decisions did Kalp Bajpai personally make while designing, running and improving the programme?

The pilot is not designed to manufacture scholarship claims. Its purpose is to create real product-learning evidence that may later support scholarship applications if the results are meaningful.

## Recommended cohort

- Curriculum: CBSE
- Grade: Class 10
- Subject: Mathematics
- Target enrolled: up to 120
- Target completed: at least 100, if realistically achievable
- Partners: 2–5 independent schools, tutoring centres or education organisations
- Duration: four weeks
- Suggested cadence: three structured Pri Learning sessions per week

## Safeguarding and privacy

- Obtain appropriate school/organisation permission before recruitment.
- Where required for minors, obtain parent/guardian consent.
- Collect the minimum personal information needed to operate the programme.
- Use anonymised student IDs in analysis and scholarship evidence.
- Do not publish identifiable student data.
- Participation must be voluntary and students must be able to withdraw.

## Evaluation design

### Baseline

Create a syllabus-blueprinted assessment covering the same content domains as the post-assessment. Record:

- anonymised participant ID
- baseline percentage
- content-domain performance
- date completed

### Programme

Students complete structured Pri Learning mathematics practice over four weeks. Where the product supports it, capture:

- active days
- completed sessions
- questions attempted
- topics attempted
- feedback/hint interactions
- correction attempts
- misconceptions or error categories
- completion status

### Post-assessment

Use a parallel assessment with matched syllabus coverage and similar difficulty, but not identical questions.

Capture:

- post-assessment percentage
- content-domain performance
- student feedback
- teacher/partner feedback

## Interpretation rules

A simple pre/post programme does not prove causation. Acceptable language includes:

- "Participants' average assessment score changed from X to Y during the programme."
- "Students who completed at least N sessions showed an average change of Z percentage points."

Do not claim:

- "Pri Learning caused a Z% improvement"
- "The programme scientifically proves Pri Learning is better than tutoring"

unless a future study uses an appropriate comparison design and statistical analysis.

## Kalp leadership log

Kalp should create at least one dated entry per week using this structure:

| Date | Problem | Decision | Rationale | People coordinated | Action | Outcome | What failed / changed next |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Useful leadership evidence comes from concrete decisions, not titles.

## Independent verification

At least one external partner should be able to verify:

- Kalp initiated or led the programme
- the approximate number of participating/completing students
- the programme actually ran
- Kalp communicated with students/teachers/partners
- any major changes made in response to feedback

## Final deliverables

By 17 October 2026, produce:

1. anonymised pilot dataset
2. summary metrics and charts
3. partner verification letter(s)
4. Kalp leadership decision log
5. Pri Learning product-change log
6. 3–5 page Pri Learning India Student Impact Brief
7. claim-to-evidence index for scholarship use

## Scholarship integrity rule

Every number or achievement used in a scholarship application must have a corresponding source in the evidence index. If a claim cannot be verified, do not use it.
