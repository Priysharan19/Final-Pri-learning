# Pri Learning India Student Impact Pilot — Matched Assessment Bank

**Bank version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Use with:** `pilot-assessment-protocol.md`

## Administration rule

The partner selects a fixed set of 8–10 module IDs that students have already been taught before baseline testing. The same module IDs are used on both forms. Do not select modules after seeing student results.

Each item is worth 2 marks. Preferred assessment: 10 items, 20 marks, 30 minutes.

Participants with anonymous IDs ending in an odd digit use **A → B**; participants ending in an even digit use **B → A**.

# FORM A — ITEM BANK

## A1 · Real Numbers

Given

\[
540=2^2\times3^3\times5,
\]

find the least positive integer by which 540 must be multiplied so that the product is a perfect square. Show the prime-factor reasoning.

## A2 · Polynomials

The zeroes of \(x^2-7x+10\) are \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\). Without solving the quadratic, find

\[
\alpha^2+\beta^2.
\]

## A3 · Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

Solve simultaneously:

\[
x+y=7,\qquad x-y=1.
\]

## A4 · Quadratic Equations

Solve:

\[
2x^2-5x+2=0.
\]

## A5 · Arithmetic Progressions

For the arithmetic progression

\[
5,9,13,\ldots
\]

find:

1. the 20th term; and
2. the sum of the first 20 terms.

## A6 · Triangles

In \(\triangle ABC\), points \(D\) and \(E\) lie on \(AB\) and \(AC\) respectively, and \(DE\parallel BC\). If

\[
AD=3\text{ cm},\quad DB=2\text{ cm},\quad AE=4.5\text{ cm},
\]

find \(EC\).

## A7 · Coordinate Geometry

Point \(P\) divides the line segment joining \(A(2,3)\) and \(B(8,15)\) internally in the ratio

\[
AP:PB=1:2.
\]

Find the coordinates of \(P\).

## A8 · Introduction to Trigonometry

For an acute angle \(\theta\),

\[
\tan\theta=\frac34.
\]

Find \(\sin\theta\) and \(\cos\theta\).

## A9 · Some Applications of Trigonometry

From a point on level ground 20 m from the base of a vertical tower, the angle of elevation of the top is \(45^\circ\). Find the height of the tower. Ignore eye height.

## A10 · Circles

From an external point \(P\), tangents \(PA\) and \(PB\) are drawn to a circle. If

\[
PA=2x+3\quad\text{and}\quad PB=3x-2,
\]

find \(x\) and the common tangent length.

## A11 · Areas Related to Circles

Find the area of a \(60^\circ\) sector of a circle of radius 6 cm. Give the exact answer in terms of \(\pi\).

## A12 · Surface Areas and Volumes

A solid is formed by placing a hemisphere of radius 3 cm on top of a cylinder of the same radius and height 4 cm. Find the total volume of the solid in terms of \(\pi\).

## A13 · Statistics

The grouped data below have class intervals 0–10, 10–20 and 20–30 with frequencies 2, 4 and 2 respectively.

Find the mean using class marks.

## A14 · Probability

A fair six-sided die is rolled once. Find the probability that the number shown is prime.

## Scoring integrity notes

- Accept mathematically equivalent exact forms.
- Do not award a second mark for an unsupported final answer when the item explicitly requires reasoning and the partner's normal marking practice expects working.
- Do not change one form's rubric independently of the matched form after baseline data are seen.
- If an item is later found genuinely defective, invalidate that matched module for **both** forms for the entire cohort and document the reason before aggregate analysis.
