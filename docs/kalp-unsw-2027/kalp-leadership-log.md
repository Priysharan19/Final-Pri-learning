# Kalp Bajpai — Leadership Evidence Log

Use this contemporaneously from 6 September 2026 onward. The goal is to prove decisions and responsibility, not manufacture a retrospective founder story.

## Rules

- One entry whenever Kalp makes a material decision; minimum one substantive entry per week during the pilot.
- Link to objective evidence where possible: issue, commit, partner email, meeting note, deck, dataset version or product screenshot.
- Record failures and reversals. Mature leadership evidence is stronger when it shows learning rather than only wins.
- Do not attribute team work to Kalp unless he personally led or completed the stated part.

## Strong scholarship examples to extract later

Only after evidence exists, convert selected entries into this structure:

**Situation:** What real problem existed?

**Responsibility:** What was Kalp accountable for?

**Decision:** What did he personally decide or lead?

**Evidence:** What independently proves the action/result?

**Reflection:** What did he learn and what changed next?

## Never use

- 'I led' when Kalp only attended
- 'I built' for team work without specifying his contribution
- 'impacted N students' when N is registrations rather than completed/meaningful participants
- 'improved learning by X%' when the pilot only observes pre/post change
