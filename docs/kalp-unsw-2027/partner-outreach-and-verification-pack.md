# Pri Learning India Student Impact Programme — Partner Outreach & Verification Pack

## Objective

Recruit 2–5 credible schools, tutoring centres or education organisations for a four-week CBSE Class 10 mathematics pilot led by Kalp Bajpai, while creating independent evidence suitable for later scholarship use.

## Partner outreach email

**Subject:** Free four-week CBSE Class 10 mathematics pilot — Pri Learning

Dear [Name],

My name is Kalp Bajpai. I am a Class 12 student and [Founder/Co-Founder — factual title only] of Pri Learning, a mathematics-learning project focused on personalised practice and feedback.

I am leading a small four-week CBSE Class 10 mathematics pilot and would like to explore whether a group of students from your school/centre could participate at no cost.

Students would complete a short baseline assessment, use Pri Learning for structured mathematics practice during the programme, then complete a parallel post-assessment and a short feedback survey. The purpose is to understand how students use the product, where they struggle and what we need to improve.

We will collect only the minimum information needed, use anonymised participant IDs for analysis, follow your organisation's consent/safeguarding requirements, and will not publish identifiable student data.

If useful, I can share the one-page programme outline and discuss the practical requirements with you.

Kind regards,
Kalp Bajpai
[Founder/Co-Founder], Pri Learning

## One-page programme outline

### Cohort
CBSE Class 10 Mathematics.

### Duration
Approximately four weeks.

### Student workload
- baseline assessment
- approximately 3 structured Pri Learning sessions per week
- parallel post-assessment
- short feedback survey

### What the partner provides
- appropriate permission to invite students
- parent/guardian process where required
- a staff contact
- confirmation that the programme ran and approximate participation/completion numbers
- optional teacher feedback

### What Pri Learning provides
- no-cost access to the pilot
- programme instructions
- baseline/post assessments
- student support for product use
- anonymised summary results

### Data principles
- data minimisation
- anonymous IDs in scholarship/research analysis
- no publication of identifiable student information
- withdrawal respected
- no causal claims from the simple pre/post design
