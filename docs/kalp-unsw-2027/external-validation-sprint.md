# Kalp Bajpai — External Validation Sprint (September–October 2026)

Checked on 6 September 2026.

## Objective

Obtain at least one credible external innovation/entrepreneurship signal before the UNSW scholarship submission, without sacrificing Class 12 academics or fabricating new activities.

## Priority A — Youth Ideathon 2026

- National entrepreneurship/innovation competition for school and college students in India.
- Classes 8–12 are eligible.
- Students may enter individually or in teams of up to five.
- Participation is free.
- Pri Learning fits the EdTech/Open Innovation framing naturally.

### Submission positioning

Do not submit Pri Learning as "an AI app that will revolutionise education".

Use this structure:

1. **Problem** — students often receive right/wrong outcomes without useful feedback on mathematical reasoning.
2. **User** — Indian secondary-school mathematics students.
3. **Current solution** — Pri Learning is being built as a personalised mathematics-learning system with structured practice and feedback.
4. **Kalp's role** — include only verified personal responsibilities.
5. **Evidence** — use real pilot/product metrics only.
6. **Learning** — explain a real limitation discovered and what changed.
7. **Scale** — explain how better personalised feedback could become more accessible across India.

## Priority A — GITAM Innovation Challenge 2026 Junior Track

- submission receipt
- final pitch deck/video
