## 3. The AP / ECF policy as the marker applies it

Source of truth: `docs/product/marking-first-error-ecf.md` (hashed above). In short:

- A line earns only as the proof of a stage, and each stage earns at most once. Restated givens, bare values, prose, the copied question and padding earn nothing.
- A substitution stage is proved only by a line showing the authored expression with the numbers in it, never by the bare value or another computation reaching it.
- The first mathematical break is marked once. A later line that is correct on the student's own wrong value is "carried forward": it is not called wrong. Under `ecf: 'method'` it may earn its method stage; it never earns the answer mark.
- A substitution into a wrong formula never earns. A `supporting` stage (the last term on a sum question) earns only when a primary method stage is verified.
- Method marks are capped at one below the question's marks, so a wrong final answer never earns full marks, and a one-mark question pays no method mark. A right final answer earns full marks under the existing final-answer policy.
- Marking is online-only and server-authoritative (ADR-0001 amendment 2026-10-10). A model never sets a mark.
