## 4. Marking conventions — each to be confirmed by the examiner

Summarised in Pri's own words from `docs/research/NSW_HSC_CURRICULUM_ASSESSMENT_AUTHORITY.md`. **None is confirmed.**

- **`nsw-convention:final-answer-only`** (research §5.2, §8.3) — Prithagoras pays a question's marks for a right final answer and nothing otherwise. Working is read and shown back as feedback, never marked. HSC marking rewards relevant reasoning (research §5.2), so the app must say plainly it does not give HSC-style working marks. *To be confirmed by the examiner.*
- **`nsw-convention:equivalent-forms`** (research §5.1 ("or equivalent merit")) — A final answer is accepted in any form the engine proves equal to the key (fractions, decimals at the precision asked, equivalent expressions). The examiner confirms the forms accepted for each answer type are those an HSC marker would accept, and lists any that should be refused. *To be confirmed by the examiner.*
- **`nsw-convention:rounding-tolerance`** (research §5.2) — Cells whose tolerance is wider than the precision the question names (for example a cent or a dollar of drift) are NOT issued until the examiner rules on what rounding drift, if any, an answer may carry. They are listed in section 6. *To be confirmed by the examiner.*
- **`nsw-convention:multiple-choice`** (research §5.2) — A multiple-choice item is marked on the option chosen, one mark, as in Section I of an HSC paper. *To be confirmed by the examiner.*
- **`nsw-convention:no-method-marks-from-india`** (research §8.3) — No CBSE (#499) certification carries to NSW (ADR-0002 §6). Method marks for Prithagoras need this jurisdiction's own certification, per syllabus version, by at least two independent certifiers (research §8.3). *To be confirmed by the examiner.*
