# Person 1 deterministic test boundary

`person1-intelligence-loop-check.mjs` is a software-contract regression suite. Its fixtures are authored and deterministic.

It must never be reported as:

- real-writer handwriting accuracy;
- physical-iPad latency evidence;
- real-student misconception prevalence;
- proof that every curriculum question type is covered.

Those claims require their own measured datasets. The suite exists to prove that already-validated subsystem outputs are connected safely and that an upstream uncertainty/error cannot be hidden by downstream intelligence.
